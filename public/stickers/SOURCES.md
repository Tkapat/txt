# Desk art sources

All desk art in `public/stickers/` and `public/sketches/` was generated for this
project. No third-party artwork is shipped.

- `stickers/sticker-01.webp` … `sticker-07.webp` — generated scrapbook pieces
  (note cards, lined note, tag, butterfly, badge, button, daisy), cut to
  transparent WebP with `scripts/slice_stickers.py`.
- `sketches/leaves.webp`, `flower.webp`, `circuit-leaf.webp` — generated
  graphite pencil sketches on a transparent background.

Licence: original work created for txt, distributed with the project.
